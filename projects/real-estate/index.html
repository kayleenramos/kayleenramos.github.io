About this Neighborhood, School System, and Community

Wellesley is a town in Norfolk County, Massachusetts, United States. It is part of Greater Boston and is located 15 miles west of downtown Boston. The town is home to several parks, recreational facilities, and cultural institutions, making it an attractive place to live for families and professionals alike.

Wellesley is also known for its historic architecture, with many well-preserved homes and buildings dating back to the 19th and early 20th centuries. The town has a strong sense of community, with numerous events and activities throughout the year that bring residents together.

Community events and festivals are held throughout the year, such as the annual Wellesley Festival of Arts, fostering a strong sense of belonging among residents.

Schools in Wellesley rank among the best in the state, providing excellent educational opportunities for students.
